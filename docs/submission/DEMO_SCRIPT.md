# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Record on the phone running the v0.2.6 APK, with screen recording on. Show only what you have seen working on that phone. If a shot fails, cut it rather than describe it.

## Before recording

1. Install v0.2.6, finish onboarding, and download **Qwen3 1.7B** in Models (the safest choice for 3 to 4 GB phones). Wait until the chat banner disappears.
2. Telegram: create a bot with @BotFather, get your chat ID (message @userinfobot), then in Settings > Telegram enter both, save, and turn on **Reply to my Telegram messages**. Send `/start` from Telegram and confirm a reply.
3. Check-ins: in Settings > Assistant, turn on **Gentle check-ins**. To have a nudge ready for the video, create a task, then tap **Snooze** on it three times (or start and stop its focus session early three times). "Not the right step? Try another" does not count: asking for a new step is treated as progress. Close the app and wait 15 to 30 minutes, phone unlocked, outside 22:00 to 08:00. Keep the notification for the video. If it does not arrive, leave this shot out.
4. Optional: Settings > Assistant > **Notice phone-use patterns**, then allow Beginora in the Android screen that opens.

## Shots

| Time | Show | Say (short) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Title card or your face | "Starting a task is one of the hardest parts of ADHD. Beginora is an AI assistant that runs on your phone and helps you take the first step." |
| 0:15 | Turn on **airplane mode** on camera | "Everything you see next runs offline, on the phone." |
| 0:20 | Today tab: tap **Start with one small step**, type "I need to start my essay", tap **Make it smaller** | "I describe a task. A small language model on the phone turns it into one first step I can start in about 30 seconds." |
| 0:45 | The task opens with its first step; tap **Start 5 minutes** | "There's a calm five-minute focus session. No streaks, no guilt. Stopping early is fine." |
| 1:10 | Back on the task, tap **Not the right step? Try another** | "If the step doesn't fit, it suggests another." |
| 1:25 | New task: type "how much medication should I take", tap **Make it smaller** | "Safety comes first: crisis or medical questions get a fixed, reviewed answer and never reach the model." |
| 1:40 | Pull down the **check-in notification**, tap it, the task opens | "Beginora doesn't wait to be opened. In the background it notices a task that seems stuck and sends one gentle note, never at night." |
| 2:00 | Settings > Assistant: show the switches and their text | "Everything proactive is opt-in. Phone-use patterns read app names and times only, and nothing leaves the phone." |
| 2:15 | Turn airplane mode off. Telegram: send `/add study for my exam`, then `/next`, then `/done` | "Where data is scarce, you can use it from Telegram. Only your own chat gets answers." |
| 2:40 | Settings > About disclaimer, then title card with the GitHub link | "Beginora is a planning aid, not medical care. The code and tests are on GitHub." |

## Rules

- Keep it under the length limit on the Devpost page; check it before recording.
- No claims that Beginora treats, diagnoses or improves ADHD.
- Blur or crop the bot token and your chat ID if they appear on screen.
